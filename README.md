# Steal a Slime

A collect-and-steal tycoon for Roblox. Why this game: see [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md). What players say about similar games and how we answered it: [FEEDBACK.md](FEEDBACK.md).

Buy slimes off the conveyor or roll the Slime Dispenser, they earn cash every second and grow bigger (even offline), raid other bases to steal theirs, shield your own, rebirth for permanent multipliers.

**What's in it**
- 32 slimes across 9 rarities (Common to Secret, plus code-only Exclusives), each with its own look: crowns, wings, halos, horns, antennae, auras and glow.
- Mutations: Golden (x2), Lava (x3, Lava Storm), Diamond (x4), Candy (x5, Candy Party), Rainbow (x8).
- Traits (stack up to 3, multipliers add up): Sparkly, Spooky (October), and event traits Midas, Meteor, Sugar, Rich, Clover. During an event, meteors strike slimes already in your pens and can give them that event's trait.
- Slime Dispenser: pay cash for a random slime, odds shown on the board next to it. 5x Luck boosts Rare+ odds (free for 10 minutes for new players, from codes, during Slime Rain, or bought).
- Codes menu: `RELEASE`, `SLIMETIME`, `LUCKY`, `FREESLIME`, `SPOOKY` (Halloween, until Nov 1). Add more in `Config.Codes`.
- Slime Index: collection book, +1% income per slime discovered.
- Merging: 3 identical slimes (hold R) fuse into one ★ slime with 3.5x income, up to ★★★.
- Daily rewards: 7-day login calendar (cash, luck, Grow Boost, and the exclusive Sunny slime on day 7). Missing a day restarts it.
- Raiding: thieves glow red with a THIEF tag; if the owner catches them, the slime bounces its way home.
- Effects: hopping belt slimes, bouncing pens, rarity reveals, confetti, screen flashes, camera shake, Slime Rain drops, sounds.
- Halloween update: Pumpkin, Ghosty, Vampire spawn only in October.
- Gear shop (cash): Slap Hand (free), Speed Coil, Gravity Coil, Slime Trap (Rebirth 1), Slime Taser (Rebirth 2). Slapping or zapping a thief knocks your slime loose and it bounces home.
- Server events every 8 minutes: Golden Hour (Golden x10), Lava Storm (Lava mutation x3), Candy Party (Candy mutation x5), Cash Frenzy (2x income), Lucky Hour (3x luck). The world changes color and things fall from the sky.
- Playtime gifts: 7 presents that unlock from 2 to 60 minutes into a session, including free Dispenser rolls.
- Daily quests: 3 new goals a day (buy, roll, collect, merge, steal, slap, gifts), plus a bonus for finishing all three.
- Friend boost (+10% cash per friend in the server, up to +50%) and Premium boost (+10%).
- Fuse Machine (next to spawn): put in 3 slimes plus a fee, wait 10 minutes, get one slime whose rarity is based on the average you put in. Odds are shown before you fuse, and the best mutation is kept half the time.
- Base upgrades (permanent, kept on rebirth): Slime Food (+15% income per level), Stronger Shield (+15s lock), Bigger Base (+1 pen).
- Player levels: XP from everything you do, cash every level, luck every 5 levels, titles over your head from Slime Rookie to Slime Legend.
- Index milestones: finding 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30 slimes pays out automatically.
- Squirming slimes: carried slimes wriggle, and the thief has to tap HOLD ON (or press F) in time or it slips free and bounces home. Rarer slimes squirm more.
- Revenge: get robbed and for 90 seconds the thief's shield can't stop you, you carry at full speed, and they glow red for you.
- Bounties: every steal puts a WANTED bounty on the thief; slap, zap or trap them to claim it.
- King Slime: a co-op boss every 12 minutes. Slap it with the whole server; everyone who helped gets paid and the top slapper gets a lucky roll.
- Rookie Shield: new players can't be robbed until they've earned $3K, rebirthed or stolen themselves.
- Nemesis: whoever steals from you twice becomes your Nemesis. Only you see the ☠️ over their head; you run 20% faster near them, their bounty pays you +50%, and stealing one back is an AVENGED! moment for the whole server.
- Slime Snacks: 5 snacks (Berry, Pepper, Honey, Crystal, Glowcap) drop from rolls, belt buys, quests, bounties and the King, or buy them in the 🍽️ Snack Bar. Feed 5 of one kind to a slime (hold G) and it evolves into a diet form (Juicy, Spicy, Sweet, Gem, Glowing) with a new look and x1.4 to x2.2 cash. A Diet Book counts every form you've made.
- Blackout: every 25 minutes the lights go out for 90 seconds. Free base locks stop working (bought shields and the Rookie Shield still hold), you get a flashlight, pen slimes glow, and the Gloom drifts through the street. If it touches you, you drop what you carry and wake up at home.
- Base Relics: 12 rule-changing charms (Common Crowd, Star Polish, Insurance, Sticky Floor, Lucky Charm, Quick Fuse...). Equip 3, they float above your base, and they reset on rebirth so every run is a new combo. Income relics add up, never multiply.
- Bases: 4 per side with gardens between them. Every base starts with a wood floor, posts, glowing entrance pillars, a rug and hedges, and gets nicer at rebirths 1-10, 12, 15, 20 and 25 (stone, flowers, lanterns, a gate, crystals, marble and gold, fireflies, banners, party lights, sparkles, a golden statue, a floating crown, a glowing floor, rainbow trim).
- Event drops: during every event themed boxes fall into the street and the first player to touch one gets it (Golden Crates, Lava Rocks, Candy Boxes with snacks, Money Bags, Clover Boxes with luck, and Glow Orbs with Glowcap snacks in a Blackout). Themed boxes can also give one of your pen slimes the event's trait. 8% are 🎁 Lucky Boxes (free roll 50%, big cash 30%, lucky roll 15%, relic 5%). Up to 8 pickups per player per event.
- Mini games at the ends of the street, for while you wait on a slime:
  - 🎣 Goo Pond: cast from one of 8 decks, wait for the bite, tap REEL in time. Catches are cash, snacks, luck, a free roll or (1%) a relic; odds are on the board.
  - 🔨 Whack-a-Slime: 2 machines, 30-second rounds. Click or tap slimes (golden = 3, bombs = -2). Score pays cash, 20+ adds a snack, 30+ adds luck. Server record on the screen.
  - 🗼 Sky Tower: a 38-step spiral jumping course with bounce pads. Touch the start pad, reach the chest at the top for a prize (every 15 min, 10% relic chance). Fastest climb is shown.
- ⛈️ Weekly Slime Storm: every Wednesday 23:00 UTC and Saturday 17:00 UTC (7 PM / 1 PM US Eastern), on every server at once, for 20 minutes. Storm-only slimes (Stormy, Thunderbolt, Tempest), 3x luck, 2x cash, ⚡ Charged trait strikes (x5), Storm Crates in the street, the King Slime, rain and lightning. Warnings at 10, 5 and 1 minute, and a countdown chip in the HUD. Change the times in `Config.Storm`.
- First-time guide: new players get 3 pinned goals with a glowing arrow (buy a slime, collect cash, lock the base), then a free roll. Tips after that come one at a time.
- 🌟 Slime Season: a free 30-tier reward track every calendar month (UTC). Season XP comes from quests, bounties, the King, mini games, steals, rebirths and more; tiers pay cash, luck, snacks, lucky rolls and relics, and tier 30 is the exclusive Starling slime. Tune it in `Config.Season`.
- Friends: 💌 INVITE button opens Roblox's invite screen.
- Save safety: each save is locked to one server, so fast rejoins and server hops can't lose or duplicate slimes.
- Analytics: onboarding funnel (joined, bought, collected, locked, done) and events for steals, rebirths, mini games, the King, fusing and evolving. See them in Creator Hub > Analytics once the game is live.
- Global leaderboards by spawn: Top Earners and Top Thieves (they fill in once the game is published).

Store art: `marketing/icon_512.png` (game icon) and `marketing/thumbnail_1920x1080.png` (thumbnail).

## 1. Test it in Studio (10 minutes)

1. Install Roblox Studio and sign in with your Roblox account.
2. Open `StealASlime.rbxl` (File > Open from File).
3. **Home > Game Settings > Security:** turn on *Enable Studio Access to API Services* so saving works in tests. (Studio asks you to publish first; you can do step 2.1 below, then come back.)
4. Press **Play**. Buy a Goop off the belt (hold E), stand on the green pad to collect, try the red shield button.
5. To test raiding: **Test > Clients and Servers > 2 Players > Start**.
6. To try the paid features without paying: set `Config.StudioOwnsAllPasses = true` in `ReplicatedStorage > Shared > Config`. Set it back to `false` before publishing.

## 2. Publish (needs your account; I can't do this part)

1. **File > Publish to Roblox**. Name: *Steal a Slime*. Genre: Simulation. Description: see below.
2. **Game Settings > Places > Max players: 8** (there are 8 bases).
3. Fill the experience questionnaire in Creator Hub (maturity: Minimal) so it shows to all ages.
4. Make it **Public** in Creator Hub > your experience > Configure.

Suggested description: *Buy slimes, grow them into giants, and STEAL the best ones from other players! Lock your base before thieves strike. Slime Rain every 15 minutes: rare slimes x5!*

## 3. Turn on monetization

Creator Hub > your experience > **Monetization**:

- **Passes**: create these four, then paste each ID into `Config.GamePasses`:
  2x Cash (199), VIP (299), Extra Pens (149), Auto Collect (99).
- **Developer Products**: create these seven, paste IDs into `Config.Products`:
  5x Luck 15 min (79), Small Cash (25), Big Cash (99), Huge Cash (399), Shield 5 min (39), Grow Boost (49), Server Slime Rain (199).
- Prices in Roblox must match `price` in Config (Config's price is only what the shop button shows).
- Republish (File > Publish to Roblox) after pasting IDs.
- Also turn on **Private Servers** (Monetization > Private servers, e.g. 100 R$/month) and Premium Payouts happen automatically.

Need icons? Any square 512x512 image works; a screenshot of a glowing slime is fine to start.

## 4. Getting players (the hard part)

New games get almost no traffic by default. In order of cost:

1. **Free:** post short clips of steals/rare drops on TikTok/YouTube Shorts (this is how Steal a Brainrot blew up). Tell friends; first players matter for the algorithm's retention signal.
2. **Sponsored ads in Creator Hub** (Ads Manager): start with ~1,000-2,000 R$ ($10-25) to get first data. Watch Day-1 retention in Creator Hub Analytics; aim for 15%+ before spending more.
3. **Update weekly**: a new slime or event each week. Games that stop updating drop fast.

## 5. Numbers to watch (Creator Hub > Analytics)

| Metric | Healthy target |
|---|---|
| Day-1 retention | 15%+ |
| Day-7 retention | 5%+ |

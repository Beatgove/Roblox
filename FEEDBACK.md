# What players say about steal and tycoon games, and what we did about it

Research from October 2026: reviews, dev posts and patch notes for Steal a Brainrot, Grow a Garden and Roblox tycoons in general. Most "reddit" results now point to SEO spam, so this sticks to sources that quote players or developers directly.

## What players complain about

| Complaint | Where it came from | What Steal a Slime does |
|---|---|---|
| Losing your best item hurts, and kids cry and quit | Kotaku review; Brainrot changed its own drop rules after a 67% Discord poll | **Squirm** gives thieves a chance to fumble, **Revenge** lets you hit back, and slapped slimes already bounce home |
| New players get robbed before they've learned the game, or get scammed | Brainrot newbie guides | **Rookie Shield**: no one can rob you until you've earned $3K, rebirthed or stolen yourself |
| "Done everything in 40 minutes" | Kotaku | Traits, Fuse, upgrades, levels and Index rewards (last update), plus the King Slime and bounties now |
| Paying Robux to escape traps feels cruel | Kotaku | Nothing in the shop gets you out of a trap. Traps last 4s, and every paid random reward shows its odds |
| Events felt rushed, and players wanted old ones back | Grow a Garden dev statement (Gameranx, PC Gamer) | All 5 events rotate forever (never the same twice in a row), and the King Slime is its own regular event |
| Rare spawns stolen in busy servers | Brainrot guides | The Dispenser roll is personal, so nobody else can grab it |
| Thin combat ("just a sword") | Roblox DevForum | 5 gear items, now with bounty targets and a boss to use them on |

## What players love (and we lean into)

- Simple loop, offline growth, mutations from weather and events, and constant updates (Grow a Garden).
- The thrill of a steal and the chaos that goes viral on TikTok (Brainrot). **Squirm** and the **King Slime** are made to be clipped.
- Social play and gifting with friends. The friend boost is in. Trading is a later step, and Grow a Garden had to rebuild its backend to stop duplication exploits before shipping it.

## What makes Steal a Slime different now

1. **Squirming slimes.** Stolen slimes fight back. While a thief carries one it can wriggle, and the thief must tap HOLD ON in time or it slips free and hops home. Rarer slimes squirm more, so the best steals are the hardest. No other steal game has this, and it fits slimes.
2. **Revenge.** When you're robbed, for 90 seconds the thief's shield can't stop you, you carry at full speed when stealing from them, and they glow red on your screen.
3. **Bounties.** Every steal puts cash on the thief's head (a WANTED tag). Anyone who slaps, zaps or traps them claims it. Thieves become targets, and defenders get paid.
4. **King Slime.** A giant boss crashes into the map every 12 minutes. The whole server slaps it together while it hops around stomping people away. Everyone who helped gets paid, and the top slapper gets a lucky roll. Rivals end up on the same side for a minute.
5. **Rookie Shield.** New players can't be robbed while they learn the game.
